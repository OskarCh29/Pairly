# ADR-001

## Title

Relationship Journal as the Core Domain

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

The primary purpose of Pairly is to preserve and enrich the shared story of a couple.

Planning, communication, AI assistance, weather integration, achievements and every future feature exist only to support this goal.

The relationship journal is the heart of the product.

Every new feature must strengthen, enrich or preserve the couple's story.

## Consequences

- Timeline becomes the central concept of the application.
- Events are memories in the making.
- Chat messages become part of the relationship history.
- Achievements represent meaningful milestones.
- AI should encourage creating memories instead of replacing human interaction.
